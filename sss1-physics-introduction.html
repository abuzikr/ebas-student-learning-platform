TOPIC 1: INTRODUCTION TO PHYSICS

SSS 1 Physics

Prepared by EBAS Learning Academy

Learning Objectives

At the end of this lesson, the student should be able to:

  • Define physics.
  • Explain the meaning of physical science.
  • Identify major branches of physics.
  • State some applications of physics.
  • Explain the importance of physics in everyday life.
  • Identify careers related to physics.

1.1 Meaning of Physics

Physics is a branch of science that deals with matter, energy, motion, forces and the interactions between them.

Definition:

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, forces and the laws that govern natural phenomena.

Physics helps us understand how things happen in the natural world. For example, physics explains why an object falls to the ground, how vehicles move, how electricity flows and how sound and light travel.

1.2 Physics in Everyday Life

Physics is present in almost every part of our daily activities.

  • Transportation: Physics explains the motion of cars, bicycles, aircraft and ships.
  • Electricity: Physics helps us understand electrical circuits, power generation and electrical appliances.
  • Communication: Radio, television, mobile phones and satellites depend on principles of physics.
  • Medicine: X-rays, ultrasound and other medical technologies make use of physics.
  • Construction: Engineers use physics to design safe buildings, bridges and other structures.

1.3 Major Branches of Physics

Physics is divided into several branches. Some of the major branches include:

Mechanics: Study of motion, forces and the behaviour of objects.

Thermodynamics: Study of heat, temperature and energy transfer.

Optics: Study of light and its behaviour.

Acoustics: Study of sound.

Electricity and Magnetism: Study of electric charges, electric currents, magnetic fields and their interactions.

Atomic and Nuclear Physics: Study of atoms, atomic nuclei and related phenomena.

1.4 Simple Examples

Example 1:

A football is kicked and moves across the field. Which branch of physics helps us study its motion?

Answer: Mechanics.

Example 2:

Which branch of physics deals mainly with light?

Answer: Optics.

Example 3:

The study of sound belongs mainly to which branch?

Answer: Acoustics.

1.5 Practice Questions

  1. Define physics.
  2. State four areas of everyday life where physics is applied.
  3. List five branches of physics.
  4. Which branch of physics deals with light?
  5. Which branch deals with sound?
  6. State two applications of physics in medicine.

Quick Revision

  • Physics is a branch of science concerned with matter, energy, motion and forces.
  • Mechanics deals mainly with motion and forces.
  • Optics deals with light.
  • Acoustics deals with sound.
  • Physics has many applications in transportation, electricity, medicine, communication and construction.
